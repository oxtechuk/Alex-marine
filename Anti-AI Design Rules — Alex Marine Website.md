## ANTI-AI DESIGN RULES — CRITICAL

The website must look like a **real, professionally designed corporate website made by an experienced human UI/UX designer**, not an AI-generated website.

Avoid every visual pattern that commonly makes websites look AI-generated.

### 1. NO EMOJIS

Do NOT use emojis anywhere in the interface.

Forbidden examples:

❌ ⚓ 🛟 ⛑️ 🦺 🔧 🚢 ⭐ ❤️

Use professional SVG icons or a consistent icon library instead.

Icons must be:

- Simple
- Thin or medium stroke
- Consistent
- Professional
- Monochrome or brand-colored
- Appropriate for a corporate industrial company

---

### 2. NO GLASSMORPHISM

Do NOT use:

- Glass cards
- Frosted glass
- Backdrop blur
- Transparent floating panels
- Glass navigation
- Excessive transparency
- Blur backgrounds

Avoid the typical AI-generated:

"frosted glass + blur + gradient" aesthetic.

Use solid surfaces instead:

- White
- #F2F4F7
- Navy
- Very subtle borders

---

### 3. NO GLOW EFFECTS

Do NOT use:

- Neon glow
- Blue glow
- Gold glow
- Button glow
- Text glow
- Image glow
- Gradient glow
- Ambient lighting around UI elements

Buttons should have clean solid colors.

Hover effects should be subtle.

---

### 4. NO EXCESSIVE GRADIENTS

Avoid:

- Purple/blue gradients
- Neon gradients
- Multi-color gradients
- Artificial AI gradients
- Gradient text
- Gradient buttons

Use mostly flat colors from the brand identity:

#0A1D37  
#0D3B66  
#1E6FAE  
#D4A017  
#F2F4F7  
#FFFFFF  
#333333

Gradients may ONLY be used very subtly inside photographic backgrounds or large marine visual elements when they look naturally occurring.

---

### 5. NO AI-STYLE DECORATIONS

Do NOT add random decorative elements such as:

- Floating circles
- Random blobs
- Abstract 3D shapes
- Floating geometric objects
- Random lines
- Decorative dots everywhere
- Artificial particles
- 3D icons
- Futuristic HUD elements
- Cyberpunk elements
- Holographic elements
- Floating cards without purpose
- Random arrows
- Excessive waves
- Decorative stars

Every visual element must have a clear UX or branding purpose.

---

### 6. NO EXCESSIVE ROUNDED CARDS

Do not turn every section into a rounded card.

Avoid:

"card inside card inside card"

Use a natural editorial layout.

Cards should only be used where they improve:

- Product browsing
- Category browsing
- Service comparison
- Information hierarchy

Use moderate radius:

**8–16px**

Do not use extremely rounded pill-shaped containers everywhere.

---

### 7. NO HUGE AI TYPOGRAPHY

Do NOT use oversized typography just to make the design look impressive.

Avoid huge hero headlines occupying half the screen.

Typography should feel like a real corporate website.

Recommended:

Desktop Hero:

48–64px

Section headings:

32–42px

Body:

16–18px

Navigation:

14–16px

Mobile typography should scale naturally.

---

### 8. REALISTIC IMAGES ONLY

Use realistic professional photography.

Avoid:

- AI-looking people
- Unrealistic hands
- Fake workers
- Perfectly staged futuristic factories
- Unrealistic ships
- Fantasy marine environments
- Overly cinematic images
- Excessive HDR
- Artificial blue lighting

Use realistic:

- Cargo ships
- Commercial vessels
- Marine equipment
- PPE
- Fire equipment
- Rescue equipment
- Industrial environments
- Ship decks
- Safety equipment

Images should look like **real corporate photography / professional product photography**.

---

### 9. NO GENERIC AI COPY

Do not use generic marketing phrases such as:

"Empowering the future"

"Redefining possibilities"

"Where innovation meets excellence"

"Transform your journey"

"Next-generation solutions"

"Unlock your potential"

Instead, use direct and realistic B2B language.

Example:

**توريد وصيانة معدات السلامة والإطفاء والتجهيزات البحرية للشركات والمنشآت.**

The copy should sound like an actual Egyptian/Arabic marine supply company.

---

### 10. NO FAKE STATISTICS

Never invent impressive-looking statistics.

Do NOT automatically create:

"500+ Products"

"10K+ Customers"

"99.9% Satisfaction"

"20+ Years Experience"

unless the company provides these actual numbers.

If real numbers are unavailable:

- Remove the statistic
- Use factual benefits instead
- Or create editable CMS fields

Never fabricate company achievements.

---

### 11. NO FAKE REVIEWS

Do not generate fake:

- Customer testimonials
- Customer names
- Company logos
- Ratings
- Reviews
- Certifications

If real data is unavailable, hide the section or create a clearly editable placeholder in the CMS.

---

### 12. NO EXCESSIVE ANIMATIONS

Animations should feel like a normal professional website.

Use only:

- Subtle fade
- Small translate
- Image hover zoom
- Button hover
- Smooth accordion
- Simple menu transitions

Animation duration:

**150–300ms**

Avoid:

- Parallax everywhere
- Floating animations
- Continuous movement
- Elastic animations
- Bouncing elements
- Excessive scroll animations

The website should feel stable and professional.

---

### 13. NO AI-LOOKING ICONS

Do not generate custom AI-style icons.

Use one consistent professional icon system such as:

**Lucide / Heroicons / Font Awesome**

Keep icon style consistent throughout the website.

Do not mix:

- 3D icons
- Filled icons
- Outline icons
- Cartoon icons

---

### 14. REAL CORPORATE GRID

Use a professional layout grid.

Content should align consistently.

Use:

- 12-column desktop grid
- Strong horizontal alignment
- Consistent spacing
- Consistent margins
- Clear hierarchy
- Proper whitespace

Avoid random asymmetric layouts unless there is a strong design reason.

The layout should feel intentionally designed by a professional UI/UX designer.

---

### 15. REALISTIC PRODUCT CARDS

Product cards should look like an actual B2B catalog.

Each card should prioritize:

1. Product image
2. Product name
3. Category
4. Short technical description
5. Product code when available
6. Request quotation CTA

Do not use:

- Fake prices
- Fake ratings
- Fake reviews
- "Best Seller" badges everywhere
- Random percentage discounts
- Artificial urgency

---

### 16. COLOR DISCIPLINE

Use the Alex Marine brand identity consistently.

Primary:

#0A1D37

Secondary:

#0D3B66

Marine Blue:

#1E6FAE

Gold:

#D4A017

Background:

#F2F4F7

White:

#FFFFFF

Text:

#333333

The gold color must be used as an **accent**, not as a dominant UI color.

The website should primarily feel:

**Navy + White + Marine Blue**

with small Gold details.

---

### 17. NO "DRIBBBLE SHOT" LOOK

Do not optimize the design just to look impressive in a screenshot.

The website must prioritize:

- Usability
- Readability
- Product discovery
- Trust
- Conversion
- Information hierarchy
- Real business requirements

It should look like a website that could actually be deployed for a marine supply company.

---

### 18. NO UNNECESSARY SECTIONS

Do not add sections just because modern landing pages usually have them.

Every section must have a business purpose.

Prioritize:

- Products
- Categories
- Services
- Company information
- Industries served
- Product details
- Quote request
- Contact

Remove anything that doesn't help the customer understand the company or find/request a product.

---

### 19. PROFESSIONAL B2B FEEL

The final website should resemble a website built for an established:

**Marine Supply + Industrial Safety + Fire & Rescue Equipment Company**

It should feel:

- Reliable
- Established
- Technical
- Practical
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Clean
- Premium but not luxurious
- Modern but not futuristic

NOT:

- Startup landing page
- AI SaaS website
- Crypto website
- Gaming website
- Web3 website
- Portfolio website
- Fashion website

---

## FINAL DESIGN TEST

Before finalizing the design, ask:

**"If the AI-generated elements, gradients, glass effects, emojis, excessive animations, and decorative effects were removed, would this still look like a professionally designed corporate website?"**

If the answer is NO, simplify the design.

The final result must look **human-designed, commercially realistic, restrained, professional, and ready for a real marine supply company.**
